Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Processing Time

All orders are carefully packed and processed within 1–2 business days after payment has been confirmed.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

Shipping Within Malaysia

We currently ship throughout Malaysia, including:

  • West Malaysia (Peninsular Malaysia)
  • East Malaysia (Sabah & Sarawak)

Shipping fees are calculated automatically during checkout based on your delivery location.

Estimated Delivery Time

  • West Malaysia: 2–5 business days.
  • East Malaysia: 4–8 business days.

Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on courier services and destination.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you'll receive a tracking number through WhatsApp or your order confirmation page.

You can also track your parcel anytime using the Track Order page on our website.

Incorrect Shipping Information

Please ensure your shipping address and phone number are correct before placing your order.

PurelyHome is not responsible for parcels delayed or returned due to incorrect shipping information provided by the customer.

Shipping Delays

While we do our best to dispatch orders quickly, delivery may occasionally be delayed due to:

  • Public holidays.
  • Weather conditions.
  • Courier operational delays.
  • Events beyond our control.

We appreciate your patience should any unexpected delays occur.
